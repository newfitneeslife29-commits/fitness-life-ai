Configuración › Políticas → pulsa cada política → borra lo que haya → pega el texto en inglés de abajo → **Guardar**.

Decisiones aplicadas: devoluciones 30 días solo por producto dañado, defectuoso o equivocado · envío gratis · entrega 7–15 días hábiles a EE. UU.

---

## 1. Política de devoluciones y reembolsos (Refund policy)

Refund & Return Policy

We want you to be happy with your order. If something isn't right, we'll make it right.

Damaged, defective or wrong items
If your order arrives damaged, defective, or is not the item you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we will send you a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

To start a claim, email us at kavirostore.official@gmail.com with:
- Your order number
- A short description of the problem
- Clear photos or a video showing the issue (and the shipping label, if the package was damaged)

In most cases you won't need to send the item back. If we do ask you to return it, we will cover the return shipping cost.

Items we can't accept for return
Because our products ship directly from our partner suppliers, we don't accept returns for change of mind, wrong size or color chosen by the customer, or items that have been used, washed or damaged after delivery. Please check product details and size charts before ordering.

Lost or late orders
If your tracking shows no movement for an extended time, or your order hasn't arrived within 30 business days of shipping, contact us and we'll investigate with the carrier. If the package is confirmed lost, we will reship your order or issue a full refund.

Cancellations
You can cancel your order free of charge before it ships. Email us as soon as possible with your order number. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled.

Refunds
Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method. Depending on your bank or card provider, it may take 5–10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement.

Questions
Contact us anytime at kavirostore.official@gmail.com or through our contact page.

## 2. Política de envío (Shipping policy)

Shipping Policy

Free shipping
Shipping is free on all orders. The shipping cost shown at checkout is always $0.

Processing time
Orders are processed within 1–3 business days after payment is confirmed (excluding weekends and holidays).

Delivery time
Our products ship directly from our partner suppliers' warehouses. Estimated delivery time to the United States is 7–15 business days after your order is processed. Delivery estimates for other destinations, where available, are shown at checkout.

Delivery times are estimates provided by the carriers and may be longer during holidays, peak seasons or due to customs or weather delays.

Order tracking
Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with your tracking number and a link to follow your package. You can also see it in your account under Order history, or on our Track your order page. Tracking may take 2–5 days to show the first update after you receive the number.

Multiple packages
If you order several products, they may arrive in separate packages on different days. You'll receive tracking for each one.

Customs, duties and taxes
Orders shipped outside the United States may be subject to import duties or taxes charged by the destination country. These charges are not included in the product price and are the customer's responsibility.

Incorrect address
Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We are not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address provided by the customer. If you notice a mistake, contact us right away and we'll try to update it before the order ships.

Lost or damaged packages
If your package is lost or arrives damaged, see our Refund & Return Policy or contact us at kavirostore.official@gmail.com.
